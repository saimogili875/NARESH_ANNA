About the college

Built for the two years that decide the next twenty.

Founded in 1992, Sri NRI Junior College began as a single-block science college with dedicated classrooms and state-of-the-art laboratories. Today it runs four full streams across two campuses, but the founding idea hasn't moved: intermediate is not a waiting room before "real" college — it's where habits, confidence and direction get set.

We keep sections small enough that a subject teacher notices when a student who used to sit in the front row starts sitting at the back. Board results matter here, and so does the six weeks before NEET or JEE when a student needs someone to just be available.

01

Small sections, real attention

Capped at 40 per section, with weekly progress notes home for every student.

02

Entrance-exam integrated

MPC and BiPC timetables are built around JEE/NEET prep, not bolted on after hours.

03

Labs students actually run

Physics, chemistry and biology labs are hands-on from week one, not demo-only.

04

A counsellor, not just a warden

Every student is assigned a mentor faculty they can walk up to without an appointment.

Admissions

How admission works, start to finish.

Step 1 · Jun

Submit the application

Fill the form online or collect a physical form from the admissions office. 10th-grade marksheet required.

Step 2 · Jun

Stream counselling

A short conversation with a faculty counsellor to confirm the stream fits your goals, not just your marks.

Step 3 · Jul

Document verification & fee payment

Bring original certificates for verification; fee can be paid in full or in two instalments.

Step 4 · Jul

Orientation week

Campus tour, lab safety briefing, and a first meeting with your section's mentor faculty.

What you'll need

  • 10th-grade marksheet (original + 2 copies)
  • Transfer certificate from previous school
  • 4 passport-size photographs
  • Aadhaar / ID proof of student
  • Caste / income certificate, if applying under quota
